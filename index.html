Courses and Tees

A course needs a rating and a slope for each set of tees — both are printed on the scorecard. The rating is the score a scratch golfer is expected to shoot; the slope (55–155, usually near 113) is how much harder it plays for everyone else.

The League Handicap

The calculation the app shows first, and the one the leaderboard is ranked on. It applies to every golfer here — one rule, so the table compares like with like. The official World Handicap System figure is always worked out alongside it and never changes.

Golfers

Each golfer keeps their own rounds and their own handicap. Courses are shared between everyone.

Share a Read-Only Link

Anyone opening this link sees the handicaps you pick, with no way to change them and no sign-in. The rounds travel inside the link itself — nothing is uploaded anywhere, and opening it doesn't touch whatever they have saved in their own browser.

Which Golfers

Pick more than one and the link opens on the leaderboard. The courses their rounds were played on come along automatically — a score means nothing without the rating and slope it was worked out from.

How Many Rounds

Nothing is deleted — this only decides how much of your history rides in the link, which is what keeps a long one sendable. A trimmed link shows trimmed figures: both handicaps are worked out from the rounds inside it, so they can differ from the ones on your screen. Whoever opens it is told which window they are looking at.

Your Link

This is a snapshot, frozen as it looks right now — rounds logged later won't appear, so send a fresh link when the numbers move. There is no way to withdraw one once sent, so treat it like emailing a spreadsheet. The league rule rides along, because it is what these figures were worked out with; nothing identifying does — no email, no account, not even your theme.

Back Up & Restore

One JSON file holding every golfer, course, round and setting — a copy you keep, independent of this browser and of any Google account. With no account required to use the app, clearing your browser data is the one thing that will lose your history.

Restoring replaces everything in this browser. You are shown what the file holds and asked to confirm first — and if you are signed in, the restored copy becomes the one your other devices get.

Start Again

Deletes every round, course and golfer. The league rule and your theme are kept.
